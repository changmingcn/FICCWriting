# Digest extra fields (FICC)

T0 pastes this section verbatim into every digest packet, after the digest template. It does not change the template; it only adds fields to every FACTS row and a few constraints on how to write. The packet's State names the trunk question and the role and expected use of the question this source serves (FICCHand 3.1 ⑥); read that line before the source.

Record every load-bearing figure (FACTS row) in full, once; where an item is missing, write “原文没给” and name whose original it is:
- the indicator's full name; measurement basis: stock or flow, gross or net, transaction basis or valuation adjustment, coverage (all or one category, private or official included or not); units as in the original, not converted by hand; time basis, sample, period, base period, denominator;
- who calculated it: the original's author, management, media retelling, a filing, or our own calculation (mark “我们自算”);
- vintage: where statistics were revised or a table's definition changed, state which version (example: from a certain quarterly vintage of Z.1 on, hedge funds are listed separately);
- holders, custodians, channels: whether people, accounts or institutions are counted, and what is mixed into a balance;
- dates: the date printed on the original (cover, masthead, disclosure appendix) > the source-list date > the file name's collection date (write “M月D日收录”); “as of” is the data cutoff date, not the release date; do not infer the release date from the file name; dates outside the current year carry the year;
- anchor: `S<源编号> L<原件行>`, line numbers down to the line.

How to write:
- Three layers: what the original wrote, retelling (“据X”), our inference (marked “我们推断”); hedge words at the original's strength.
- Attribute a view only to the house whose original sentence said it; plural speakers stay plural; do not put a dataset's name in an institution's mouth.
- Quote the whole sentence: the second half of the same line is often a reversal.
- When the same institution says different things at different times, record a version chain (date plus original words); do not treat it as a misquote.
- Two reports from the same house on the same day: tell them apart with a short title each.
- For what the material does not say, write “原文没说X”; do not write it as a denial, and do not fill in for the original.
- Names follow the original's terms; do not coin names.
