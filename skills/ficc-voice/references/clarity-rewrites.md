# Clarity rewrite examples

All material below is made up to illustrate method; it is not market fact. Structure settles the relations first; Voice makes the reader see them. Do not memorize sentence patterns. First identify what each change preserves.

## 1 Full rewrite example: break out the meanings first, then rewrite by relation

The material and the relation sheet below are both made up to demonstrate expression methods; they do not represent actual market conditions.

**Settled task and relations.** 上级要判断“基准利率下降能否立即降低所有企业的存量贷款利息”。上一节点已讲新贷款可以按较低基准利率定价；本节点补上限制：存量贷款须看利率类型及重定价时间。子项按同一维度比较X公司与Y公司；两家公司共同说明传导存在时间差，不比较最终节省金额。

**Material.** 评估时点为2026年10月。假设基准利率下调1个百分点，各笔浮息贷款的加点保持不变，浮息贷款将在各自下次重定价时降低利率。X公司存量贷款中，80%为到2028年到期前利率不变的固定利率贷款，20%为2027年1月1日重定价的浮息贷款。Y公司的存量贷款全部为浮息贷款，2026年11月1日重定价。没有两家贷款余额，也不假定到期后如何续贷。

**Mixed version.** 新贷款利率会下降，X的固定浮息结构和Y的月度贷款重定价安排使企业融资成本对降息的反应存在差异，前者80%到2028年，后者下个月，20%下季度，因此利息都会下降。

**Meanings broken out.**

- 共同情景：基准利率下调1个百分点，浮息贷款加点不变。
- X的固定利率部分：占80%，到2028年到期前利率不变。
- X的浮息部分：占20%，2027年1月1日重定价。
- Y的贷款：全部为浮息，2026年11月1日重定价。
- 本块能说明传导时点；没有余额，不能计算两家节省多少利息。

What is broken out here are units of understanding, not the final number of paragraphs. Put the shared scenario near the summarizing judgment. X's two sub-items both belong to X; state each clearly. Compare Y with X on the same dimension. The relations and the claim both come from the settled relation sheet.

**Rewrite.**

新贷款可以按较低的基准利率定价，但存量贷款的利率能否下降、何时下降，要看合同的利率类型和重定价时间。以2026年10月为评估时点，假设基准利率下调1个百分点，各笔浮息贷款的加点保持不变。

在这一情景下，X公司仍有80%的贷款利率在2028年到期前保持不变。这80%的贷款是固定利率贷款。其余20%为浮息贷款，要到2027年1月1日重定价时才会降低利率。

相比之下，Y公司的贷款全部为浮息，2026年11月1日重定价时就会降低利率。因此，在同一降息情景下，Y的全部存量贷款会先降息，X则只有浮息部分稍后降息。X的固定利率部分在到期前不变。两家的存量贷款余额未给出，不能据此计算或比较节省的利息金额。

**Check.** The first paragraph gives the classification and the time frame first. Both of X's parts stay inside the X item, and Y is compared on the same dimension. Every rate-cut statement is still inside the shared assumption. 80%, 20%, 2028, and the two repricing dates each have a clear object. “到期前不变” (unchanged before maturity) has not turned into “到期后必降” (sure to fall after maturity). The close does not fill in the unknown savings amount. The rewrite is longer, but the reader no longer has to find objects for three times or match consequences to two kinds of contract.

**Another sentence that may stay as it is.** “如果基准利率下降且加点不变，浮息贷款会在重定价时降息，但固定利率贷款在约定期限内不变。” The condition, the two kinds of object, and the contrast are all clear. It does not have to be split just because it has several clauses.

## 2 The overall judgment must hold every sub-item

**Relation sheet:** 上级要说明“总量不足以判断所有家庭的偿债压力”。本节点先说总体负担下降，再说某个群体仍承压，两项构成转折。

**Known material for this example:** 两项指标均比较2024年与2025年；居民债务收入比分别为100%、90%，低收入家庭逾期率分别为3%、4%。

**Unclear version:** 居民偿债压力已经缓解。债务收入比从100%降到90%，低收入家庭的逾期率却从3%升到4%。

**Rewrite:** 居民总体债务负担下降，但低收入家庭仍承压。居民债务收入比从2024年的100%降到2025年的90%。不过，同期低收入家庭的逾期率从3%升到4%。

The paragraph opening holds both sides first, and the indicators that follow support their own judgments. The time basis comes from this example's known material. When the original has no point in time, verify it first. Do not copy dates from the example to fill the gap.

## 3 Sequence must not be turned into cause

**Known material:** 公司在6月上调报价，7月订单减少。没有证据说明订单为什么减少。

**Do not rewrite it as:** 公司上调报价，所以订单减少。

**You may write:** 公司6月上调报价，随后7月订单减少。现有材料还不足以判断，订单减少是否由提价引起。

If the relation sheet nonetheless says “提价导致订单减少” (the price rise caused the drop in orders), first report the evidence gap and hand it to Structure to clarify. Adding a “所以” (so) does not add a mechanism.

## 4 Pair conditions, outcomes, and caps item by item

**Settled terms:** A组同时满足登记和缴款条件才能获得分配，金额不得超过其未偿余额；B组只须登记，分配额为固定的10万元。

**Unclear version:** A、B两组登记或缴款后分别按余额和10万元获得分配。

**Rewrite:** A组须完成登记并缴款，才能获得分配，分配额不超过该组未偿余额。B组只须完成登记，即可获得10万元。

“并” (and) and “或” (or) differ. “不超过余额” (not exceeding the balance) does not equal “等于余额” (equal to the balance). After splitting the sentence, still keep the condition, party, amount, and cap for each item.

## 5 Write each party next to its own obligation

**Settled content:** X公司负责偿还贷款；Y公司只对其中200万元提供保证。

**Unclear version:** X和Y分别负责贷款偿还和200万元保证。

**Rewrite:** X公司负责偿还贷款。Y公司只对这笔贷款中的200万元提供保证。

Write both companies and both responsibilities out in full. They must not be merged into “两家公司共同承担贷款” (the two companies jointly bear the loan). “只” (only) and “其中” (of which) are information too.

## 6 Only with the same words is the reference stable

**Unclear version:** 银行把债券卖给基金后，它的现金增加了。这种改善降低了风险。

**Settled relation:** 卖出债券使银行收到现金，缓解的是银行短期支付压力；不讨论基金和其他风险。

**Rewrite:** 银行把债券卖给基金，收到现金后，短期支付压力有所缓解。

Make clear who gets the cash and which pressure eases. Do not leave a vague “风险降低” (risk falls) for the reader to guess. When the material proves only that cash increased, do not add a further effect on your own.

## 7 Keep notes apart from argument

**Pure note:** 本段只讨论一个利差指标如何变化，下句不依赖它的英文别名。 Put the alias in parentheses where the word is first used.

**Argument step:** 某指标统计范围不包含小企业，因此不能据它判断所有企业的融资状况。 Here the scope limit decides the conclusion. Write it into the body text. Do not hide it as a note the reader can skip.

The test is whether later text depends on this information, not how long the explanation is. How to hang it: FICCVoice.md 5.1.

## 8 The writer does not decide an unclear relation

**Relation sheet:** “两组数字支持总判断。”

**Needs clarifying:** Which judgment do they support? Are the two sets of figures the same indicator for different groups, the same group at different points in time, or evidence for two different mechanisms? Which step does each complete?

When the relation is already clear, the writer may, within the authorization, adjust word order and split sentences and paragraphs to make it show. When the relation is not yet decided, list the gap and hand it to Structure. Do not pick whichever interpretation sounds smooth first.
