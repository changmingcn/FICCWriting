# Lecture terms: sources, referents, and where they must not be substituted

Use these terms only in lecture notes, tutorials, and case lecture scripts, and only when following that lecture's definitions. When writing a monthly letter, or citing official statistics or accounting statements, use the names of the statistical measurement basis or of the statement itself. The rule in FICCVoice.md 5 that “存款、准备金、资本、现金和流动性不混用” (deposits, reserves, capital, cash, and liquidity are not used interchangeably) applies here too: that 《熊猫笔记》 says “备付金” does not mean reserves or cash elsewhere may be called that. Lecture terms also change. Before continuing a lecture line, first check the term rulings in the change log at the end of that piece, and follow the latest version.

| Term | Source | What it means (which party's which kind of asset or liability) | Where it must not be substituted |
|---|---|---|---|
| 备付金 | 《熊猫笔记》, money and banking chapter (“基础流动性——我们把基础流动性叫做备付金”) | Base money held by commercial banks, used for clearing and for sustaining credit expansion | When citing official statistics (“准备金余额”, “超额准备金”) or accounting statements, use their own names. L14 version 5 has changed “备付金” in that piece to the asset-side “流动性缓冲” |
| 信贷收支表 | 《熊猫笔记》, money and banking chapter (“如何根据信贷收支表理解 Fiat Money 的信贷流动机制”) | A commercial bank's balance sheet: the name the lecture notes use when stressing the credit-creation mechanism | When the accounting statement itself is meant, or when citing regulatory indicators, use “资产负债表” (balance sheet) |
| 阻尼 | 《熊猫笔记》, money and banking chapter (“每一步都有阻尼——商业银行信贷创造的阻尼是存款准备金和资本约束”) | The constraints on credit creation: for commercial banks, deposit reserves and capital; for shadow banks, the collateral ratio | Only for the constraints on credit creation. Do not use it for market frictions or transaction costs in general |
| 无人区 | L14 (“Pozsar 管 30 天到一年之间那段叫‘无人区’”) | The wholesale funding maturity range from 30 days to one year, between the two regulatory lines LCR and NSFR | Only for this maturity range. It is Pozsar's term; give the source at first mention |
| 原始存款 | L14 version 5, figure 1 caption (“第一层是原始存款，不是本体系贷款创造的、从体系外面送进来的外币存款”) | The first layer of a bank's liability side: foreign-currency deposits sent in from outside the system, not created by this system's lending | Not the same as M0 in monetary statistics, and not deposits in general. The old name “M0 存款” (M0 deposits) is retired |
| 主动负债 | L14 (“银行主动发欠条换来的，所以叫主动负债”) | The second layer of a bank's liability side, the IOUs the bank issues itself: certificates of deposit and bills, interbank, repo, FX swaps, bonds | Only for this layer of bank liabilities. Do not use it for borrowing by firms or governments |
| 流动性缓冲 | L14 version 5, asset-side section (“流动性缓冲（联储账户、代理行账户、可售美债），商业银行真正能动的美元”) | The three tiers on a bank's asset side that can be drawn on at any time: Fed account, correspondent-bank and Tokyo clearing-bank accounts, saleable US Treasuries | Not the same as regulatory HQLA or the LCR numerator. When citing regulatory indicators, use the indicator's own name |
| 后盾表 | L14 version 5 (“后盾表：私人后盾、日本央行操作、财务省储备、联储互换线，有，但不在这张信贷收支表上”) | The four off-balance-sheet rows of backstops: private backstops, Bank of Japan operations, Ministry of Finance reserves, Fed swap lines | Not counted in the bank's own assets and liabilities, and not added to the liquidity buffer |
| 地基 | L14 (“地基是有利率的地基”) | A metaphor for original deposits | A metaphor, not a term. In another lecture, state again what it refers to |

Retired old names: “M0 存款” (M0 deposits), “真钱” (real money), and “四层负债” (four-layer liabilities) are terms of L14 versions 3 and 4. On 2026-09-15 the user ruled that terms be reviewed against classical money and banking, and version 5 has changed them: “M0 存款” became “原始存款”, that piece's “备付金” became the asset-side “流动性缓冲”, the former “央行那一层” (the central-bank layer) moved off the table into the “后盾表”, the uses of “真钱” and “四层负债” were deleted, and “层级” (tiers) is used only for the three buffer tiers and the backstop table. “杠杆池” (leverage pool) and “第零层到第三层” (layer zero to layer three) do not appear in the version 5 body text. The word list of the old language style (2026-09-03) predates this ruling and no longer serves as grounds.

Other terms in the money and banking chapter that lecture notes and case lecture scripts may keep using: 贷款创造存款、Debit 和 Credit 同时发生、扩表／缩表／收表、抵押率、资金流动图 (used interchangeably with Flow Chart)、结算行的在岸账户、离岸货币银行系统放在与企业等同的层级、在岸／离岸、套息套汇、搬运 (moving base money between onshore and offshore).

How to check: each Source cell quotes an original sentence from the lecture notes, which can be found with grep in the L14 version 5 body text or in the 《熊猫笔记》 compilation. Handle new terms the same way. Where the original sentence cannot be found, write “定义待作者补” (definition to be supplied by the author).
