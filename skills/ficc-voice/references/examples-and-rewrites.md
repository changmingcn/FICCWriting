# Examples and revision examples: what each section of the three main files looks like

This file only shows what things look like; it sets no rules. Since 2026-10-11, the example sentences, revision examples, author's original sentences, tree-diagram examples, and drafts the user edited by hand from the three main files, FICCOutline.md (outline), FICCStructure.md (position and relations), and FICCVoice.md (clear statement), are all moved here, filed by the main file's name and section number, in the same order as the reading order of those three files. The main files still govern the rules; when an example and a rule disagree, change the example. 〔九节〕 (Nine Sections), 〔熊猫讲期权〕 (Panda on Options), and 〔某年某月月报〕 (monthly report of a given year and month) mark the author's original sentences, for reference, not to be compared against; the parentheses after them give the line number (source notes: see the Appendix). 〔改例〕 (revision example) marks sentences and audits from the drafts of 《1009AI表外》, giving only the structural problem and the direction of the fix; since 2026-10-13 it also takes the faulty sentences of the first draft of 《1012散户》 and the rewritten sentences actually published in its final version (written 散户v1 and 散户, followed by the line number; Codex# is the item number in the sentence-by-sentence list that picked out the sentence), and the faulty sentences in the final versions of two other 1012 pieces with illustrative rewrites that only move material and delete no facts (written 海外 and 企业杠杆, followed by the line number; the illustrative rewrites were not published). 〔亲改〕 (user's own edit) marks drafts the user edited by hand; where the original sentence comes from one of the 1012 pieces, the short title and line number are given (e.g. 居民杠杆:25). 〔例〕 (example) marks examples written for this file, and since 2026-10-14 also sentences in final versions that were written right (e.g. 海外:205). Example sentences for connectives are in references/connectives.md 4 and 5 and are not repeated here. Revision examples of the methods of clear expression: see references/clarity-rewrites.md; this file keeps the record of actual revisions, synced with the current rules and section numbers, and the author's original sentences are not changed.
