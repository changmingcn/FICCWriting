# Conversion to written register and faithful rewriting

Based on the experience, endorsed by the user, of the 2026-09-22 FXProject revision. Use it for transcript cleanup, removing colloquialism, and tasks that change only the prose. New articles use the written register of FICCVoice.md and do not, on that account, apply the original-text protection procedure. The written register may be raised; information density, content, and judgments are not reduced along with it.

## 1 Change the expression, keep the explanation

Read through first. Identify whether the author is narrating facts, stating a view, setting a condition, restating someone else's view, or explaining a mechanism with a hypothetical scenario. Then revise the parts that sound colloquial. Do not rely on searching for colloquial words and replacing them one by one.

- Turn classroom cues such as “大家记住” (everyone remember) and “下面我讲个故事” (now let me tell a story) into natural cohesion. Pure cue phrases may be dropped, but the points, emphasis, and pointers they carry must be kept. If “请看下一页” (please see the next page) does locating work, keep it or change it into an explicit reference that the original material supports. Do not invent figure numbers.
- Join “你可能注意到……为什么？答案是……” (you may have noticed … why? The answer is …) into an observation and an explanation. An open question stays open, and a guess stays a guess. Do not answer for the original just to turn it into a declarative sentence. Posed questions that do analytic work, and existing posed-question headings, may stay.
- Tidy offhand additions, loosely strung sentences, and repeated restarts into coherent paragraphs by reorganizing within sentences, making references explicit, and using fitting connectives. In a task where the content stays unchanged, keep explanations, cases, conditions, and necessary restatements. Do not compress the whole text into a summary of conclusions. When the structure stays unchanged, do not split or merge paragraphs.
- Change colloquial exaggeration into an expression of equivalent meaning and degree. Avoid toning everything down to “较” (rather) or “可能” (possibly). Keep useful financial metaphors, concrete trades, and hypothetical scenarios. Changing “栗子” to “例子” (example) is enough; do not delete worked examples or generalize them into abstract assessments.
- Distinguish classroom role-play from the author himself. “如果你是一家基金的经理，面临……” (if you were a fund manager facing …) may become “假设某基金经理面临……” (suppose a fund manager faces …). A real “我曾与交易员讨论” (I once discussed with traders) keeps its first-hand attribution, and “我们估计” (we estimate) keeps its nature as an estimate. Actual quotations, and historical public statements kept as quotations, stay as in the original even if they contain colloquialisms. Hypothetical teaching dialogue may be paraphrased, but its hypothetical nature and the attribution of views must be kept.

Below is a hypothetical example paragraph newly written to illustrate register; it is not real market action or the author's experience. Each version is one paragraph and keeps the same event, cause, inference boundary, and author estimate.

Original expression:

> 大家看，一家基金明明看好债券，怎么反而卖了？原因其实很简单，它最近要付赎回款，可手里的现金不够，只能先卖掉一部分容易成交的债券。所以你看到卖单，也不能就说它不看好了。我们估计，如果赎回继续增加，它可能还得再卖。

Written expression:

> 这家基金虽然看好债券，但近期需要支付赎回款，手头现金又不足，只能先卖出部分易于成交的债券。因此，不能仅凭卖出行为就认定它已不再看好债券。我们估计，如果赎回继续增加，它可能仍需卖出债券。

## 2 The places where meaning is most easily changed

The following are diagnostic examples compiled from this experience. They are not verbatim quotations from the original, nor a replacement table for the whole text.

| Original meaning | What to keep, and common mis-edits |
|---|---|
| 如果产业蓬勃发展，融资需求会增加 | Keep the condition and the original strength of inference. Do not change it into “产业蓬勃发展带动融资需求增加” (thriving industry drives up financing demand), which turns the supposition into fact |
| 可能形成约束；会产生影响；很强 | Keep possibility, certainty, and degree respectively. Do not change them uniformly into “产生约束” (creates a constraint), “可能产生影响” (may have an effect), or “较强” (fairly strong) |
| 净额看起来不大；账面被改写 | The former must not become “净额波动有限” (net fluctuation is limited), and the latter must not be shortened across the board to “账面重估” (book revaluation). Keep the limit of the observation and the scope of the concept |
| 收入留在本地；控制哪些因素、控制到什么程度 | Do not change them into “收入来自本地” (income comes from local sources) or leave only “控制因素的数量” (the number of controlled factors), lest the relation or the dimension change |
| 市场有一种说法认为汇率必然贬值 | Keep the attribution and strength of the view under discussion, even if the author is criticizing it. Do not weaken it on the other side's behalf into “存在贬值风险” (there is depreciation risk) |
| KOSPI 每跑赢 10% | If the original states no comparison baseline, do not add “全球指数” (global index). Flag missing or doubtful information separately. Do not fill it in on your own during a rhetorical revision |

Keep the direction and force of parallel judgments separately. “倾向于推高资产，同时倾向于压低汇率” (tends to push up assets while tending to push down the exchange rate) must not have one side changed to certainty while the other stays possible. “常说” (often said) and “常见” (often seen), “差异明显” (the difference is marked) and “差异出现” (a difference appears), are not interchangeable synonyms either.

## 3 Comparison and delivery

Read the revised draft against the original paragraph by paragraph. Check whether content was added or removed, who is doing what, whether cause and condition are the same, whether the author's or others' judgments still have clear attribution, and whether affirmation or negation, degree, point in time, and scope of application have changed. Keep figures, units, formulas, variable symbols, English terms, and citations. Historical text is not updated to the current measurement basis just because it is converted to written register.

Apply structure protection as the task requires. When the task requires leaving the structure alone, check headings, levels, order, paragraph boundaries, tables, images, footnotes, and cross-references. Links and controls in native documents must be kept too. Matching figures and keywords and equal paragraph counts cannot replace a semantic read.

Bold should fall on the complete point or term it corresponds to after the rewrite. Do not carry formatting over mechanically by old offsets or by the longest common fragment, leaving a lone “的／是／与”, a truncated word, or missing a key judgment such as “方向相反” (opposite directions). When a deleted pure cue phrase has no semantic counterpart, do not move its bold onto an unrelated subject. Choose the document tool and the specific checking method by file type. Do not turn one project's script or batch arrangement into a required step of writing.
