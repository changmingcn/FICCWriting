At-hand drafting summary: the rules the composer and the writer use, one per line, each line ending with its home rule (since 2026-10-17, the home rule of a paragraph-organization item is in FICCCompose.md and that of a sentence item in FICCVoice.md). When the home rule changes, change its line here at the same time.

1. At every layer the conclusion comes at the opening: for a paragraph, look at the first sentence; for a paragraph group and above, the first paragraph; for a subsection of one to three paragraphs, the first sentence. When the first sentence only sets the tone, the second sentence carries straight on and completes the view. A story, a scene, or a post-mortem of one's own errors may follow the course of events and put the judgment at the end, but the opening still says what the story shows. (FICCCompose.md C01, C02)
2. Arrange a carry-over as a chain that goes deeper, marking what further step each step takes; one that reports only the route, coordinates, or counts is a table of contents and must be rearranged. (FICCCompose.md C04)
3. A carry-over arranges only the trunk and states only conclusions, without numbers or examples; counter-views, the conclusion section, and appendices do not go into the carry-over. (FICCCompose.md C05, C06)
4. Open each block with a linking-back sentence that ties back to the previous block's conclusion, with a relation word or a back-reference, then give this block's claim; parallel blocks need links too. (FICCCompose.md C13)
5. The whole piece and each part need a close; a section needs one when it has three or more child nodes; below section level a close is optional. A section made of several numbered stories needs an overall opening and a close, however many stories there are. A close gathers the child nodes back into the claim and then goes one step further; it does not restate in other words; a close that gathers several strands gathers all of them. Do not force carry-overs and closes onto small nodes to fill out a format. (FICCCompose.md C17, C19)
6. A child node's opening re-mentions its step from the parent node's carry-over with one more layer, not copied word for word. (FICCCompose.md C07)
7. Each paragraph does one main thing for the layer above. The first sentence is the paragraph's claim or topic; a topic says what state the subject is in right now, not “下面讲X” (next, X). The first sentence is not a material sentence (what some institution said, a number); move the conclusion at the paragraph's end up to its opening; hook openings and stories follow item 1. The sentences after it are all branch lines around the first sentence (a branch line may divide further), and between branch lines of the same layer holds one of eight relations: parallel, progression, turn, supposition, alternative, cause and effect, condition, sequence. A note is not a branch line; how it hangs: FICCVoice.md 5.1. For two adjacent paragraphs, you can say what was advanced. At the start of the paragraph's opening sentence and of each node's opening sentence, show with a connective or a named back-reference its relation to the level above and to the sibling it connects to (usually the previous one; when it connects to an earlier one, the back-reference names it); its subject is the thing this sentence judges (subject: FICCVoice.md 4.4). Do not open with an embedded question. First write what the gap means for the judgment, with what the material does not say as the reason (except in paragraphs that analyze the material itself; FICCVoice.md 4.4). (FICCStructure.md 2.1; paragraph openings and paragraph tasks FICCCompose.md C01, C09; relation at sentence start and the subject FICCVoice.md V22, 4.4)
8. A sub-point gives its conclusion first, then facts, numbers, and examples; after giving a number, say what is worth seeing in it here. The figure, the figure note, and the nearby explanation must match: the figure must not show one object while the body text uses it to prove another. (FICCCompose.md C08, C22; figure title and figure note FICCVoice.md V31)
9. Every item after a colon or a dash answers the sentence before it, and all items serve the same judgment; merely adding unrelated detail about the preceding object does not count as an answer. (FICCVoice.md V19)
10. Mark every same-layer seam that has a relation, including those between parallel items. A paragraph-opening connective links to the previous paragraph's claim or close, not across an inserted sentence or a route sentence. Turn (light turn included), cause and effect, conditions and limits, and concession must be marked; re-mentioning the previous paragraph's words does not count as marking (FICCVoice.md V22; word choice: FICCVoice.md 4.1). (FICCVoice.md V22, V53)
11. Merged into FICCVoice.md V22.
12. As many steps, and such relations, as the carry-over sentence previews, the first sentences of the sub-points pay off with the matching markers. (FICCCompose.md C20)
13. When one seam carries two layers of relation, mark the layer the relation sheet names (FICCStructure.md S29); when two layers nest across two seams, the outer layer's word goes at the outer seam and the inner layer's word at the inner seam. (FICCVoice.md V54)
14. Do not write what happened in sequence as cause and effect, or what happened at the same time as mutual reinforcement. (FICCVoice.md V21)
15. The same conclusion appears at most twice in the openings of adjacent layers. (FICCCompose.md C07)
16. Use “” for quotation marks and ‘’ inside; do not use 「」『』 (home rule: format-and-maintenance.md 1).
17. Within a paragraph, set out the points first and hang on each point the sources that support it; make the point the subject and put the sources after it; do not arrange by “哪家说了什么” (who said what). (FICCVoice.md V27)
18. Across sections, refer back by name; across articles, refer by issue number. When citing others, the body text hangs the institution and the date on the point (date to the day, so the original report can be found by date), in parentheses at the end of the sentence or as an adverbial of that number; report titles go into the source list. The body text carries a short title only when one institution has two reports on the same day that must be told apart, or when the title itself is the point to be made. Keep the speaker in the sentence (“X说”, X said) only in the cases listed in FICCVoice.md 5.1. How to write source details: FICCVoice.md 5.1. (FICCVoice.md V27)
19. When the same company changes role along the chain, state its role in this sentence whenever you write about it. When you say “损失” (loss), “赔” (pay out), or “扛” (bear), say which party; do not merge several parties into one subject. Check: circle every “赔”, “损失”, “扛”, and “低估” (underestimate), and ask which party is its subject. (FICCVoice.md V15)
20. An added claim, carry-over, or close sentence only brings forward and gathers back conclusions this block already has; it does not elevate them and does not supply reasons the original lacks. A rewrite adds no facts; keep the original judgment's attribution and degree words at their original strength. Whether to keep the speaker and how to hang the source: FICCVoice.md 5.1. (FICCOutline.md 7.3)
21. Copy the author's original sentences marked 〔作者亲写·逐字〕 in the outline word for word; do not change a character. (FICCOutline.md 7.3)
22. Word choice, terms, names, and grammatical person: per FICCVoice.md. Relations: per the relation sheet. Position, order, and length: per the outline. Paragraph organization and level of detail: per FICCCompose.md. When a relation is missing, contradicts itself, or conflicts with the material, list the specific gap and send it to T0 for Structure to clarify; write the conflict-free part first; do not invent relations or write a known error as given. (SKILL.md, FICCStructure.md 6)
23. Body text carries no “待补充” (to be supplied) or “待核实” (to be verified) labels: write a fact to be verified as a sentence without it, or leave a blank; the labels go only into the result sheet. (FICCStructure.md 6.1)
24. The measurement basis goes with the numbers it compares, a qualifier with its judgment, sources and evidence with the statement they support; do not hang them at the paragraph opening or in the lead. (FICCStructure.md 2.4)
25. When several nodes need the same fact (a number with its indicator name and base period, a definition, a judgment), the outline names one node to state it in full; the other nodes (the lead, the summaries at each layer, the closes, the tables in changed sections) write only the conclusion, with at most one number, and refer back to the place that states it in full. A fact for which the outline sets no full-statement place is stated in full by the node that uses it, not only referred back to. (FICCOutline.md O51)
