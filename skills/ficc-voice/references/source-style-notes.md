# Style observations on the original texts

Source: the user-designated 《文稿.docx》 (private Google Doc; ID withheld). The text was fetched through the connector on 2026-09-05: about 286,000 characters, including several years of monthly letters and fairly colloquial long articles. What follows rests on a close reading of representative sections. It does not claim to have reviewed every piece of the collection.

It is style material, not a database of financial facts. Use excerpts only to recognize techniques of expression. Historical judgments, and the simplifications, figures, and regulatory interpretations in them, must still be verified per the writing task.

## 1 Reusable writing methods

**2018.7: choose your own question out of a real debate.** The opening discusses the debate over national fortunes, policy efficiency, and China–US relations, then comes down to entrusted funds and to the mechanisms by which macro stories affect strategy. What is worth learning is explaining why this question is studied. There is no need to invent conversations with peers in every piece, nor to copy its emphasis on macro and fund flows.

**2018.11: acknowledge common sense first, then check the conditions.** The original accepts the usual explanation that expected returns attract funds, then asks whether costs are fully counted and whether collateral and risk capital are sufficient, then uses the China–US rate spread to discuss concrete fund channels. The argument advances because each step corrects the scope of application of the step before it.

**2019.3: correct concepts with transaction chains and analogies.** The description of wealth management products, trusts, broker channels, and share pledges lets the reader see why simple aggregation may double-count. Then the analogy “不能因为财政部增加国债发行就认为财政部是国债空头” (you cannot call the Treasury a short seller of Treasuries just because it issues more of them) explains why a borrower sells. Learn the relations. Do not generalize the original's judgments of that time. This issue also has “总结一下” (to sum up), which shows that the old version's absolute ban on summaries was not faithful to the model texts.

**2023.03: first put the reader in a participant's position.** The original explains the bank's services with the scenario of venture capitalists whose funds and projects are out of step, then covers deposit inflows, securities investment, clients drawing down deposits, and asset sales. The scenario is not decoration but the starting point of the balance-sheet changes that follow. Absolute statements about risk conclusions and the consequences of the takeover should not be inherited as general facts.

**2024.11: the error itself becomes the argument.** The original says plainly that its past RMB judgment was “错的离谱” (wildly wrong), then explains that the willingness of multinationals and financial institutions to maintain the old model has changed. What is worth learning is a truthful post-mortem of the conditions the error rested on, not writing every piece as a confession, nor inventing a failure for the author.

**2025.12: “券商的血包” (the brokers' blood bag) has a corresponding financial channel.** This passage first explains the node where the standby funds sit, then unfolds the relation between money market funds and brokers. “肥腩” (fatty belly), “血包”, and literary associations bring vividness. What is worth learning is unfolding the imagery around a single mechanism, not transplanting all the metaphors, strong assertions, figures, and institutional explanations as they are. This piece mixes persons, lists, and colloquial speech. Do not derive from it surface rules that the whole collection must follow uniformly.

**2026.01: push a research view forward to the conditions of a strategy.** The expedition metaphor explains why a macro direction is still not enough for a complete strategy. The behavioral differences among gold buyers push the topic from aggregate demand to physical holdings and trading structure. “黄花菜都凉透了” (it is far too late by then) adjusts the tone and also carries over the tension between research time and trading timeliness. Learn this cohesion. Do not assume its specific trade advice suits other tasks.

## 2 The limits of the principles

The collection contains texts of different years, purposes, and degrees of editing. Some paragraphs string together parallelism, summaries, and strong assertions; others read more like lecture scripts. The new version, combined with the user's explicit wish to avoid AI tone, selects expression methods that work repeatedly. It does not aim to keep every sentence pattern that has ever appeared, nor does it let the surface features of the latest piece represent the whole collection. Per the user's further instruction of 2026-09-22, later writing uses the written register by default. The observations and examples below are for learning reasoning, scenes, and the author's stance. The colloquial expressions in them create no requirement to keep colloquialism.

The example below is a demonstration newly written from hypothetical material. It is not an excerpt from the original and does not describe real market action.

Material: 银行新增存款；资本约束未改善；符合授信条件的贷款需求不足；部分资金配置短债。不得推出股市有新增买盘。

Stiff version: 流动性宽松并不意味着信用扩张，其本质是结构性约束下的传导阻滞，需要关注多维变量的动态演化。

Unfolded version: 银行多了存款，企业却未必更容易借到钱。信贷部门批一笔贷款，既要占用资本，也得找到符合授信条件的客户；这两道关口没有变化，多出来的钱就可能先去买短债。于是我们会看到，银行账户宽裕了，贷款端仍然安静。资金在银行里换了一个去处，离股市新增买盘还隔着几步。

The difference is that it states clearly who faces what constraint and how the funds are therefore handled, and it keeps the boundary of the inference. It is not that a few words were swapped.
