# FICCVoice: Write each node once, from paragraphs to qualifiers

FICCVoice turns each settled outline node into paragraphs and sentences in one pass. FICCOutline.md owns placement, selection, claims, order, headings and length; FICCStructure.md owns relation types and whether a relation holds. This file owns openings, carry-overs, unfolding, payoff and closes (§1, §2) and the wording of each sentence (§3). Leave the reader nothing to take apart, pair up or fill in; split and gloss by that standard, not by a character count. No fixed sentence pattern, no deliverable table.

- Inputs: the node slice of the living `<文稿>-提纲.md` (主旨, 任务, 顺序, 接前, 分担, 边, 展开 with its cap and `本单元写` places, 段 rows), the node's 事实 sources, the signed 算式 rows, the 消歧 rows, the authorized scope and the adjacent text. Output: the packet's `思路／正文／回执`; no paragraph plan, and no paragraph outline competing with the outline for claims, grouping or order.
- Inside the scope, split, merge and re-unfold paragraphs; never change nodes, settled relations or a fixed order. Material with no place on the outline stays out of the body and goes back per V04, as do gaps and conflicts. Rewrite permissions: FICCOutline.md O69; the fidelity duties of O70 and V38 hold here too.
- Reading by duty: a leaf node reads §0, §2, §3.1, §3.2 and §4; a lead, or a node whose close doubles as its parent's, adds §1; a wording patch reads §0, §3.1, §3.2 and §4.
- “The user” is the author; “you” is the agent applying the rule.

## 0 Contract

Twelve invariants the writer accepts before writing a sentence; the rules below are their method, and V04 (when to hand back) stands outside them. Each line names its home. A check marked (fails) can fail a node; a check marked (reports) only raises a flag for judgment. Before the first sentence, answer the 动笔前三问 (I12): 写全事实有原文、非直录数有算式行、本单元写哪几处.

- **I1** **Each paragraph makes one point, and each sentence does one job for it.** Home: §2 (C01 段首款, C09). Name each sentence's job (the claim, grounds, a step, a qualifier, an element of the example, a carry-over, a close, an attributed view); a sentence with no job goes back per V04 (fails). Method: V01, V02, V03, V37.
- **I2** **The paragraph's close opens no point the opening lacks.** Home: §2 (C01 段首款, 2.5), §1 C17, C19. The last sentence introduces no point the opening lacks (fails); an echo or a consequence is allowed, not required (C17, C19), and C01's paragraph types keep their own endings.
- **I3** **A connective stands only for a settled relation, and its two sides share content.** Home: §3.1, §3.2. For every “所以、因此、但是、因为、如果” name the relation and the shared content; material that supports only juxtaposition is set side by side (fails). Method: V08, V13, V22, V53, V54.
- **I4** **A claim's qualifiers travel with it through every rewrite.** Home: §3.1. A condition stays hypothetical, a rebuttal condition or concession stays attached, a dependent finding keeps its own certainty, one hedge word keeps one strength across the piece; a qualifier the material carries but the author's passage itself omits is named and handed back per V04, never added silently, and a qualifier lost in rewriting is restored (V20) (fails). Method: V17, V20, V38.
- **I5** **The subject is what the sentence judges, and the action is in the verb.** Home: §3.1. Subject and verb stay close, the topic stays stable across a paragraph, each party's responsibility is stated separately, and merged responsibilities fail (V15 Check, V38) (reports). Method: V15, V16, V24.
- **I6** **One object, one name; one term, one meaning.** Home: the 名字表 (FICCOutline.md O32–O33), its use in §3.2. Replace each pronoun with the full name as a test, and rewrite where two objects fit or none does (fails). Method: V23, V32, V33.
- **I7** **Every number, at its first real use, says whose, which indicator, when, in what unit, on what basis.** Home: §3.1. Both sides of a comparison are comparable; where the material gives no basis, say what cannot be compared (V18) (fails). Method: V18, V26, V31.
- **I8** **The point is the subject, the source hangs after it, and a view has a holder.** Home: §3.1. No passive sourcing, no plural for one source; hits on “据悉、市场担忧、多家机构、读法、解读” are review candidates checked against the actual sources (reports); a confirmed lost holder or a plural for one source fails (V28). Method: V27, V28, V57.
- **I9** **One sentence, one judgment; punctuation carries a definite relation.** Home: §3.2. Conditions may share the sentence, a colon follows a complete judgment, body prose has no semicolons (fails: V07, and V14's matching item; the script's length flags report per V39). Method: V07, V11, V14, V19, V25.
- **I10** **The reader decodes nothing.** Home: §3.2. Notes stand apart from argument steps, a term is explained once at its first real use, a metaphor follows the mechanism with its limits stated (reports). Method: V29, V32, V35.
- **I11** **One audience, one persona, written register.** Home: §3.2; the cold-read bar: references/clarity-acceptance.md A01. A change of persona or condition is stated (reports). Method: V34, V36.
- **I12** **Check before submitting, and cold-read with the finished draft and the name table only.** Home: §4. Before writing, answer the 动笔前三问: 写全事实有原文 (each fact written in full has its source line), 非直录数有算式行 (each number not copied from a source has a signed 算式 row, V26), 本单元写哪几处 (the node's `本单元写`). A missing 算式 row: finish the attested facts and report `缺算式行｜拟求量｜操作数@锚点` in `回执`; no mental arithmetic (fails). After writing, revise by problem type (V14), check both ways against the material (V38), report problems that can be located and fixed (V40); a report missing an item fails. Method: V14, V26, V37, V38, V39, V40.

## 1 Lead, part and section openings, carry-overs and closes

### 1.1 Openings above the paragraph

- **C01** **At every layer, the conclusion comes at the opening.**
  - Give the reader first the whole needed to place the details. Replace material lists with content judgments. Do not cram all details into the first sentence.
  - Where the opening is: for the whole piece, the title plus the lead (latest landing: C03); for a paragraph group, section or part, its first paragraph. A subsection of one to three paragraphs has no claim paragraph: its first sentence (with the carry-over sentence when that only sets the tone) carries the claim. A part opening right beside the first section's gives only the part's claim and the chain of sections. Under a judgment heading the first paragraph still gives the conclusion first; under a topic or question heading the first sentence gives the judgment.
  - Paragraphs (§2 2.1): A paragraph opening must be the paragraph's claim or its topic. The first sentence not only governs what follows but also states the view itself. The second sentence then carries the main reasons when the first only sets the tone or states an argument's claim without them.
  - Every opening carries the highest signal-to-noise ratio (user ruling 2026-11-21): the lead, each part or section opening and each paragraph's first sentence, or the sentence where a later claim lands (forms 3, 4, 8), states the unit's own settled claim as 2.1 sets it. Child claims stay in the carry-over (C04, C06). Throat-clearing, sample lists, glosses and source dates move to later sentences or to the child section that owns them; secondary numbers move together with their source and date; no kept fact drops.
  - No preview that only announces the subject (“下面讲X”, “这一块讲X”).
  - Opening forms for the lead and part openings (shared row numbers 1–8; rows 1, 2, 4–7: §2 2.1):

    | Row | Form of the opening | Where the claim lands |
    |---|---|---|
    | 3 | Hook opening (lead or part opening) | Hook first, with the claim right after the hook. A hook is a price, a trade, a story, or a scene. A popular claim, a reader's question, or a real first-hand experience also counts as a hook. |
    | 8 | Long piece | It may give its judgment in two sentences: the short-term conclusion first, the long-term conclusion at the end of the same paragraph. |

  - A hook opening of an argument still carries the main reason, in the sentence that completes the claim. Hook writing: V35; payoff: C21.
  - Headings, the lead, carry-overs and the close go where the outline sets them. Rationale: references/FICCVoice-rationale.md Introduction, C01.
- **C03** **Show the whole article's claim at the start of the lead. Do not make the reader read half the piece to learn what we argue.**
  - The lead answers every 开工问 Q1…Qk of the 问题卡 with its 必带判据 (threshold, 分母, window): a stranger reading only the title and the lead can answer each one.
  - Three arrangements: situation and conflict, then the answer; the answer, then situation and conflict (both land the answer in the first one or two paragraphs); the reader's question first (what the previous issue said and readers ask, the new situation and the pessimists' view, a closing posed question, the answer as the next paragraph's first sentence). In every arrangement the main judgment shows by the first sentence of the lead's third paragraph at the latest. The pessimists' evidence list belongs to the counter-view section (FICCOutline.md O53).
  - Check: read the lead only up to where the answer lands. It gives the whole piece's claim and main reason, with no sample list, gloss, source date or roadmap (a row-3 hook sentence may carry its date). Its metric numbers are only the hook's one number, each 开工问's 必带判据 and N0's 性质与把握 probability; a ratio threshold carries its 分母.
  - Then the carry-over (C04); the hook: V35.

### 1.2 Carry-overs and chains

- **C04** **A carry-over is not a table of contents. Arrange a carry-over paragraph as a chain that goes deeper.**
  - Carry-overs: the paragraph after the lead, part and section openings, carry-over sentences inside paragraphs. A part or section opening gives in one to three sentences the step it advances on the parent's chain.
  - Each step derives from the previous one or goes one layer further, its addition marked by a connective; each trunk block takes one step, in the chain's order.
  - Lay blocks out flat only when they cannot progress (shallow to deep, appearance to cause, general to specific), or when a style-only edit or faithful rewrite inherits no chain. Flat items open with topic frames on one basis of division (volume and price, assets and liabilities, issuers and investors), marked per C13.
  - Check: a carry-over after which no overall view can be stated is a table of contents (flat blocks of one conclusion each, joined only by “另外”, “还有”); report it per V04.
- **C05** **A carry-over paragraph arranges only the trunk.** “反方看法”, the conclusion section and appendices hung after the main line stay out; linking-back sentences tie them in at their own places (hanging: FICCOutline.md O53; the whole-piece close: C18). Each step has its own conclusion; a main-line sentence the previous paragraph already said only supports this step.
- **C06** **A carry-over sits one layer above the child nodes. It states only each step's conclusion and the relations between steps.**
  - No child's own numbers, quotations or examples; before several cases, say first what they prove together. Conclusions, not routes, coordinates or counts. An upper-layer carry-over is one paragraph, never a multi-paragraph roadmap; payoff: C20.
  - Check: cover the child nodes and read only the carry-over; this block's overall view fits one sentence. Then check each step by the group's arrangement in the node's 分担 and 接前 rows, not by the seam relation: a dependency chain gives each premise before its step, a progression names the layer each step adds, a parallel group names the shared question and each item's contribution. A failing step goes back per V04.
- **C07** **A re-mention must carry one more layer. This rule governs only section level and above.** The parent's carry-over gives each trunk child one step; the child's opening may restate it more concretely only with one more layer (the conclusions or reasons of the blocks beneath). One conclusion appears at most twice in adjacent openings (the upper carry-over, this layer's claim); a close that gathers it back with something new is not a re-mention.

### 1.3 Markers between layers and signposts

- **C14** **Allocate markers between layers by scale. Above section level, let four devices together make the relations readable.**
  - At paragraph level and below, connectives mark relations fully (seam and density: V22).
  - At section level and above, lay out by genre stage (lead, trunk, counter-views, conclusion section), then mark with judgment headings (the heading alone tells the assertion: FICCOutline.md O79–O81), position (contrast, counter-views, conclusion each in place), carry-over paragraphs (one chain, each step's addition marked) and linking-back sentences (paying off the carry-over at each block's opening).
  - Relation words go in linking-back sentences, not in headings; no empty labels.
- **C15** **A signpost sentence points only to the block right after it. A stray block is reported per FICCVoice.md V04 for re-hanging; do not move it yourself.** Cross-branch foreshadowing comes from the 关键边表 endpoints (FICCStructure.md S74), carried with named content, never “留到……讲”. A block inside the main line that is not a link of it moves by the hanging rules (FICCOutline.md O53); do not patch the line with transition sentences.

### 1.4 Closes and hook payoff

- **C17** **Make every summary and close trace back to existing content.**
  - Gather only judgments this block established, with their limits; advance only as far as the material supports.
  - The close gathers the child nodes back into this block's claim and moves one step forward. Moving one step forward means saying what it implies or leaves behind, or handing the reader to the next block.
  - Do not restate the claim in other words.
  - Required closes: the whole piece, each part, a section with three or more child nodes, and a section of numbered stories (with an overall opening too, FICCOutline.md O05); below section level a close is optional. A list-style monthly report still closes the whole piece in one paragraph that gathers the sections' conclusions and moves one step forward.
  - Add no mechanical transition where the carry-over is clear; add an argument the material carries and the text lacks; what the material lacks goes back per V04. A close may first say what the matter implies, then bring the stories back to the claim; a part's close may land on method. A last child's close may double as the parent's, gathering the conclusions of all the parent's children. Paragraph closes: §2 2.5.
  - Check: against the sub-items, the opening leaves out no side and does not state partial, possible or short-term as total, certain or long-term. Extracted, the openings and closes of each layer read as one argument with no unfounded strengthening.
- **C18** **Land the whole piece's close on something grounded in earlier text. Do not recite the table of contents again.** End on a judgment, a holding condition, the next item to watch, or a maxim or ghost story grounded earlier (a summary before it, its key in earlier text); the closing sentence may gather named subtrees, then give exit conditions. A last stand-alone story needs the shared question and the main line already laid out. The ending is no place to park material with nowhere else to go.
- **C19** **Do not write every layer as “总起—分述—总之” (overall opening–itemized account–in sum).**
  - A narrative block may run by time; its parent holds the claim, and the block closes with one sentence on its meaning.
  - After a story, example, or scene, state in one sentence what it shows.
  - Cut story tails that do not lead back to the story's conclusion. Most paragraphs close on the last sentence of the last sub-point. Write “这意味着什么” only when the block derives something earlier text lacks. Force no carry-over or close onto a small node, and no close onto a life lesson the text does not infer.
- **C21** **Pay off hook details later in the text.**

## 2 Leaf paragraphs: groups, paragraphs, seams, tables and figures

### 2.1 Paragraph openings

The paragraph part of C01 (§1), its 段首款; a leaf pack reads it here.

- A paragraph opening must be the paragraph's claim (the judgment it argues) or its topic (what it is about). The first sentence not only governs what follows but also states the view itself: the paragraph's one claim. A pure enumeration names its classifying basis instead of an invented judgment. Later sentences explain, reason, illustrate, evidence or qualify it.
- The opening is the first sentence, plus the second when the first only sets the tone or gives an argument's claim without its main reasons; the second then carries them (row 1). A sub-point opens with its own first sentence; an evidence block that only supports the judgment just before it writes no claim sentence and attaches in place as 顺承 (V22).
- Highest signal-to-noise ratio (user ruling 2026-11-21): the first sentence, or the one where the claim lands (row 4), states in one sharp proposition the paragraph's own settled claim and, in an argument, the main reason, the decisive basis or threshold and any revision condition, with no direction the material lacks. Throat-clearing, sample lists, glosses and source dates move to later sentences; secondary numbers move together with their source and date; no kept fact drops.
- Replace material lists with content judgments. Never first: who said what, a number or fact that is only material (nor when every later sentence discusses it), an announce-only preview (“下面讲X”), all the details at once.
- An end conclusion (“所以……”, “可见……”) moves up as the claim and is not repeated at the end; its seams are then rewritten with reasons, evidence and qualifiers one by one, never the old material piled after a colon.

Paragraph type (decides what the opening states and whether a main reason is required):

| Row | Type of paragraph or block | What the opening states |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Argument paragraph (default) | The claim plus its main reasons. Reading only the opening, the reader knows what it asserts and on what main grounds. |
| 2 | Explanatory paragraph, or a topic stating the current state | The topic: what state the subject is in right now, such as “……有很大争议” or “……分两头”. Main reasons are not required. |
| 5 | First-hand case box | The story or facts first. |
| 6 | Story, scene, or post-mortem of one's own errors | May follow the course of events and put the judgment at the end. The opening still tells the reader what the story shows and which matter it went wrong on. Do not open empty. |
| 7 | Disagreement paragraph (the disagreement itself is the point) | The first sentence states the disagreement as the topic. The parties follow in turn, each marking its relation to the previous one (turn and so on). |

Opening form for a paragraph (rows 3 and 8: C01 in §1):

| Row | Form of the opening | Where the claim lands |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Posed question, link to earlier text, or concession first | The second sentence completes the claim (sub-items below, C13). |

- An argument paragraph opening with a hook, a posed question or a concession still carries its main reason, in the sentence that completes the claim.
- Row 4: a concession first says where the key may not lie, the second sentence where it does; a link to earlier text first corrects or ties back to the previous block's ending, the second gives this block's claim; a posed question may first tie back to the frame, and the answer follows right after it.
- Row 7: V27 and V28 keep speakers within the sentence; they govern who is the subject, not what opens the paragraph.

- **C02** **Test the paragraph opening against what follows, item by item.**
  - When both sides of a contrast or turn belong to this paragraph, make the opening hold both sides.
  - A first sentence that leaves the judgment incomplete is completed by the next, which carries the main reason as the paragraph-type table says.
  - No carry-over after an opening that states the view in full, or after a previous close that already lays out this block's question: continue with one sentence. When the lead's claim is complete and the carry-over paragraph is dropped, the lead shows the chain between parts.
  - Every sub-point the opening or carry-over sentence previews is paid off below (C20).
  - Check: attach each later judgment to the part of the opening it explains, supports, limits or rebuts. One serving only another judgment leaves the paragraph; one belonging to the settled claim but missing from the opening completes it; a claim or attachment that must change goes back per V04. Rationale: references/FICCVoice-rationale.md C02.

### 2.2 Paragraph tasks and paragraph groups

- **C08** **Build one claim per paragraph (`<=5` independent write-in-full statements pre-prose; `>=30` number strings or an unexplained basis switch post-prose triggers mandatory disposition).** Ten sentences and 400 CJK characters are review hints, not cut lines.
  - Pre-prose: each 段k row becomes one paragraph, in order, with its facts (at most five write-in-full statements). A heavy node (more than five write-in-full facts, a multi-dimension comparison grid, an 算式 or an inference) comes with 段1…段k rows (Z-PARA); one without them goes back per V04.
  - Even below 30 number strings, switching statistical universe, denominator, right-holder, or sub-question inside one paragraph requires either stating how they establish one comparison claim or splitting/tabling them.
  - Dispose of any `>=30`-number paragraph (`重段待处置`) by: (a) tabling isomorphic grids (`邻表三写一禁`, C11), (b) splitting at sub-question or basis seams while **re-carrying subject, period, basis, and governing/negative qualifiers (`拆后四带`)**, or (c) verifier-signed proof of one indivisible claim (barred when two denominators mix).
  - Unfold by the settled relation. Parallel group: each item's conclusion, then its grounds and qualifiers, one block per sub-point, and after numbers what is worth seeing. Transmission chain: each step's result is the next step's object or condition. Explanations, examples and qualifiers follow the judgment they serve, never promoted to parallel points; inside an item the layers stay apart (a sentence group per trunk, premise apart from result). “一段一棵树” does not hang every sentence on the opening.
  - One topic per paragraph. Stay in it while adding reasons, examples or qualifiers to the current judgment; subjects that together complete one comparison may share it; a sub-question that unfolds on its own opens a new paragraph within the settled relation; no isolated one-explanation paragraphs.
  - Reorganize paragraphs by relation, not by length: facts and actions share a paragraph when the reader sees how they jointly advance the argument; a turn to a self-standing question, or a switch between comparison bases, is split (with reorganization permission) at an argument-unit boundary into paragraphs that carry over. A revision stays within V04's permissions. Rationale: references/FICCVoice-rationale.md C08.
- **C09** **Each paragraph does one main thing for the layer above: put forward a judgment, explain a concept, show a scene, analyze evidence, handle an objection, form a conclusion, or drive action.**
  - Several facts and actions, one main action, shown by the first sentence with what it concludes. Adjacent paragraphs have a nameable advance: the step the later one adds. Common routes, not templates (the real relation is the node's 接前 row): judgment→explanation→grounds; story→analysis→insight; phenomenon→mechanism→impact; assertion→counterexample→revision; scene→character reaction→new action.
  - Check: a paragraph whose main action you cannot name is material stuck together; two whose advance you cannot name do the same thing. Fix both inside the node by splitting, merging or reordering (C08). A fix that would move material across nodes, change a claim or break a fixed order goes back per V04 for the lead to pass to the outline agent; a doubtful relation goes to the relation agent per SKILL.md 3.
- **C10** **Build a group of paragraphs on the same four positions.** The first paragraph is the group's claim paragraph, its first sentence the group's conclusion; the carry-over sentence chains the following paragraphs by their conclusions and relations; the middle paragraphs are sub-points; the last paragraph or sentence may close (C17) with what the group resolved and why the next group follows. Deeper unfolding uses ranked groups, leading sentences and local bullets. “第一，” sits inside a sentence (two items) or opens a paragraph, where the next sentence is the item's claim and carries information. A colon subheading (“第一个办法：”) only when each item runs to several paragraphs and needs finding again.
- **C11** **In the several paragraph groups of one subsection, each group continues to unfold around one local question.**
  - What a paragraph picks up from the previous one (its conclusion, an open question, a limit) is readable from content and cohesion.
  - A change of sub-question, statistical universe, denominator or right-holder is a cut point: split or table there (C08). Any other change of subject, point in time, measurement basis or scale of judgment is stated.
  - Next to a table (`邻表三写一禁`), the body writes only the criterion, the decisive difference and the footnote exceptions the table cannot show; it never restates cell numbers, and a fact the 提纲 marks 留表 is not retold.
  - Paragraphs that only take turns reporting institutions, financing numbers and new cases: decide first what they show together, then the order. “此外” or “与此同时” on each paragraph is not reorganization. A shared definition or background is stated once, then used concretely (placement: V32).
- **C12** **“压缩” (compress) cuts only wording and detail.**
  - A story's length, level of detail, narrative angle, and order of appearance are all decided by its task in the whole tree.
  - Where the cap cannot hold the facts the placement record keeps, return a placement objection (FICCOutline.md O58).

### 2.3 Seams and branch lines

- **C13** **At each seam, state how the preceding item connects to the new judgment.**
  - From the node's 接前 row, name the previous block's conclusion you carry over from; a child node's first sentence ties back to it by a relation word or back-reference, and the second (or the same) sentence gives this block's claim. Turning to the counter-view: tie back and turn, then the first item's conclusion at once. Between parts, summarize the previous part, then advance; between genre stages, the linking-back sentence also marks the relation. Same-kind parallel children are linked from the second item on, on top of any numbering (V08, V22); the antecedent of a linking-back sentence: V53.
  - Write per the settled relation, as actual statements, never “形成支撑、构成闭环”: cause and effect, through what action, constraint or channel the first item changes the second (only where the 接前 row or the 关键边表 carries its source anchor, FICCStructure.md S74); inference, what is known and so how far one can judge; turn or concession, what judgment the first invites and which part of it the second changes or limits; progression, the further layer it adds; parallel or comparison, the question the items answer together and their shared dimension.
  - Check: after restating both items, you can say why they connect this way; if not, hand it back per V04.
- **C16** **Before a branch line unfolds, introduce it; while it unfolds, stay on topic; when it ends, return the result.** Say why the text turns here for now, and return the result to the layer that directly governs it. A short branch returns through the meaning of adjacent sentences, without “这说明” each time; a long or many-turned one names the question the text returns to. Opposing forces may be wrapped up together, without repeating the whole piece's conclusion item by item.

### 2.4 Preview and payoff, short forms

- **C20** **As many steps as the claim and carry-over sentence preview, the sub-points pay off, in the same order.**
  - The connectives of the claim and carry-over sentence preview the sub-points' relations; each sub-point's first sentence pays off with the matching marker: after “第一……第二……”, items open with “第一，”, “第二，”; with “而且”, the second sub-point goes a layer further; with “虽然……但是……”, the conceded point comes first, then the turn opening with “但是”.
  - Check: after a paragraph, extract those connectives and compare the relations they preview with the sub-points' actual unfolding.
- **C24** **Define once, then combine in short form.**
  - When a paragraph gives the outcomes of two or three indicators, define the indicators by full name once, then write each outcome in one sentence with short names and counts (“三项都不动”, “前两项动、第三项不动”); never restate every full name in every branch, never open with a sentence that announces the outcomes will now be listed, never close with a hedge already given at the definition.
  - More than three outcomes, or outcomes that each need a qualifier, go into a table.
  - Check: count the full indicator names in the paragraph; each appears once.

### 2.5 Paragraph closes

A paragraph closes in its last one or two sentences, on a judgment, a summary or an echo, opening no point its opening lacks (I2); most paragraphs close on the last sentence of the last sub-point, and after a story, example or scene one sentence states what it shows (C19). The close gathers only what the paragraph established, never restates the claim in other words, and no numbers of unknown purpose fill the middle (C17).

### 2.6 Figures in the body text

Figure titles, figure notes, and the match among figure, note and text: V31.

- **C22** **In the body text, state what the figure shows and what to see in it here.** An introductory sentence such as “下图是……” only sets the tone.

## 3 Sentences

3.1 keeps facts, numbers, qualifiers and holders intact; 3.2 makes sentences easy to read. Every writing duty reads both.

### 3.1 Qualifiers, own calculations and inferences

#### Break the meaning down

- **V01** **State this node's task concretely.** From the node's outline slice and the adjacent conclusions, write which step of the parent's claim the node completes, which sibling it follows, and what it adds or limits. Check: “支持、递进、并列” is followed by content; a relation the outline lacks goes back per V04.
- **V02** **Split mixed meanings into statements that can be checked one by one.** Split mixed facts, causes, forecasts and bases into “谁／什么＋做什么／是什么／怎么变” items, each with its time, conditions, negations and source, and name the main judgment and each item's job for it. Delete only repetition.
- **V03** **Attach each item to the judgment it actually explains.** Each reason, piece of evidence, example or counter-evidence answers a named question; an example shows one case; correlation or sequence is not causation. Check: cover “所以、可见、而且”; if the two sides do not show why the step holds, hand it back per V04.
- **V04** **State the gap clearly, and finish the conflict-free part first.** Write the conflict-free part, then hand back (SKILL.md §3) the planned judgment or relation, how far the material supports it and what is missing; a missing 算式 row goes back as `缺算式行｜拟求量｜操作数@锚点`. Add no cause or fact, and pick no unsettled explanation for flow. A change beyond the revision grant (FICCOutline.md O69–O74), or one only reordering can meet, is reported, not made.

#### Logic between sentences

- **V13** **Make the logic between sentences explicit.** Write a relation only where the source or context supports it; supply a missing step first; never build a relation with a connective alone or turn sequence into causality.
- **V21** **Establish the content relation first, then choose the connective.** Words and relation classes: references/connectives.md 1. Keep exemplification distinct from proof, explication from progression, and supposition from condition. Do not change the relation by swapping words. Relation diagnosis: FICCStructure.md S04, S18, S27.

#### Who acts, and what each qualifier governs

- **V15** **Write the action and who performs it, then choose active or passive.** Reading a sentence, you can find who or what is in what state, did what, and mainly shows which point. Within a simple sentence, require only a clear sentence core and clear attachment of modifiers (complex sentences: V54). Turn “进行分析、承担风险” into who did what to what, and keep subject, verb and object close; from “有影响” alone add no direction or size. State a known agent that matters. If the agent is unknown, or you want to keep the patient as the topic, you may keep the passive. Name a company's role wherever it changes along the chain. Do not merge several parties' responsibilities into one subject, nor one party's statement distortion and another's debt risk into one judgment. Check: each “赔、损失、扛、低估” has one party as subject.
- **V16** **Unpack relations compressed into nouns and modifiers.** Make clear what each condition, cause, aside and source modifies: find the head of a long attributive, state the attachment the material settles (verify where it settles none), lift an embedded matter into a clause. Do not force every sentence into one subject–verb–object template. In “如果A，那么B，但只有C时才成立”, say whether C limits B or the A→B link. Place “主要、短期” and V26's binding words next to what they qualify; standard names stay whole; omit only what restores uniquely.
- **V17** **Confirm the logic of the conditions first, then write conditions and results fully paired.** Settle all-or-any and necessary-or-sufficient; write “同时满足A和B” or “满足A、B任一项”, never 、 alone; shared conditions come first, separate sets restate their subject, or use an “对象／条件／结果” table (分别: V26). “如果、一旦、只要” and their consequences stay hypothetical; “只有” never becomes “只要”, nor “不超过” “等于”. Check: every result's conditions and exceptions are found at once.
- **V20** **Check the strength of judgments before and after rewriting.** Carry each qualifier (“可能、预计、多数、约、不超过”) with its object; “并非所有” never becomes “都不”, nor “并非不可能” “必然”; missing evidence yields “无法判断”, not the opposite claim. Check: restore any lost qualifier, widened scope or changed speaker.

#### Numbers and own calculations

- **V18** **Make both sides of a comparison directly comparable.** Match definition, scope, time, base period and unit; one comparison per sentence; keep level and change, % and pp apart; name what “更高、也一样” compares against. Check: the reader can say whose what, against what, changed how; where an element is missing, say what cannot be compared, never guess the basis.
- **V26** **Write zero unattested leaf derivations (`算在树上，叶席零新数`) and keep disclosed facts, own calculations, and inferences in separate clauses.**
  - Only verbatim source figures or same-unit rounding with `约` need no `算式` row; sums, differences, shares, cross-table ratios, assumption swaps, and date roll-forwards require a signed `算式` row anchored to primary operand lines (never secondary summaries) with explicit sign/direction (`缺口／盈余`). The sentence using a row names its metric, or its denominator with a share cue; an unsigned row attests nothing. When a needed `算式` row is missing, finish attested facts and report `缺算式行｜拟求量｜操作数@锚点` in `回执`. A style-only edit computes nothing new.
  - At first real use a number says whose, which indicator, when, unit and basis, and its panel or sample source where it has one; later in the paragraph it may go by a short name of unchanged meaning; verify it against the original and leave the gaps it leaves. Two bases stand side by side with their sources, never averaged or spliced into one series. A cross-layer ratio names the layer of its numerator and of its denominator in the same sentence; with no signed row for it, write the two figures apart, never their quotient.
  - Bind every `仅／除外／或／至多／可能／而非` to its exact holder; never turn `或` into `和` or sequence into causality. A negation or disjunction over several limbs (`not A or B`, `neither A nor B`) keeps every limb; two never shrink to one. `or／at the option of／no more than` takes its 消歧 row's rendering (FICCOutline.md O51). “A、B、C分别……” with more than 3 pairs, or across two indicators, becomes clauses or a table.
  - Keep deposits, reserves, capital, cash and liquidity apart, and stock and flow, total and increment, book and market value. No “待补充、待核实” in the body: leave the slot and hand the label back (V04).

#### Sources and views

- **V27** **Make the point the subject. Hang the source after it.** Arrange by judgment, not by institution. The source closes the sentence it supports as a parenthetical (institution, date to the day), or rides as an adverbial on a number from one house's own method; titles go in the source list; lead and opening sources move to where the evidence comes. Keep the source's granularity: a monthly report is not upgraded to a quarterly one. Undated: the list's date, else the collection date marked “收录”; never guess a date part; across years, write the year. Check: rewrite “X说……X还说……” with the point as subject.
- **V28** **Do not let the attribution of views disappear in rewriting.** Keep “X说／认为” for a single view the article does not adopt, divergent views, full-sentence quotations and a house's actions such as forecasting or revising numbers; when unsure, keep the speaker. Do not strip the source from one institution's judgment and turn it into the article's judgment. Keep the source text and our inferences apart.
- **V57** **Views and inferences are not objects.** A view, reading, explanation or inference is never the subject or object of a verb that moves it: it does not “加强、变轻、占上风、站在一边、并存”, and it is not counted (“三种读法”). Write who holds it and what they believe (“<持有人>看成……”; example: references/examples-and-rewrites.md, FICCVoice V57), or what we think (“我们认为”, “在我们看来”, “更像是先后”); when several views stand side by side, write “三种说法” once and name each holder. No label for an interpretive move of ours: 读法, 解读, 视角, 框架, 判别读数 and the like are out even when the outline carries them (the outline side: FICCOutline.md O91). Check: grep the node for `读法|解读|视角|判别读数|推断加强|判断.{0,4}加强|占上风|站在.{1,8}一边` and read each hit; a hit whose subject is a company, a house, a price or 我们 passes; `voice_check.py --clarity` lists the hits as review candidates (H9), never as hard faults. 框架 is read by eye: in house drafts it is a deal or policy term.

### 3.2 Syntax, cohesion and readability

#### Length and punctuation

- **V07** **Do not use semicolons (；) in body prose.** Split each ； into short sentences with their own subjects; only table cells and genuine list lines are exempt, and prose is not turned into a list to keep them. A 〔作者亲写·逐字〕 sentence with ； goes back per V04, neither edited nor exempted.
- **V10** **Control sentence length.** Prefer short sentences while meaning and reference stay complete: cut repetition before splitting, never necessary conditions, scope or exceptions.
- **V11** **Keep each sentence focused.** Build each sentence around one main judgment or action. Conditions, scope, and exceptions that directly qualify that judgment may stay in the same sentence. If a sentence contains several judgments or actions that need separate statement, split them into several sentences, each qualifier still mapped to its judgment.
- **V19** **Make punctuation carry a definite relation.** Before ： or —— state a complete judgment that each following item answers; a ， hides no change of subject. Check: read the part before the colon or dash as a question and move any item that does not answer it. Check: after re-punctuating or splitting, reread conditions, negations and comparison targets; if their scope moved, rewrite the sentence.

#### Connectives at seams

- **V08** **Mark the add-on relation between parallel child nodes of the same kind.** At the seam C13 sets, use a repetition, emphasis or deepening marker (references/connectives.md, row 并列·附加关系); where none fits, refer back to the preceding item by name.
- **V22** **Make the relation at each seam visible to the reader, especially turn, cause and effect, condition, limitation, and concession.**
  - Do not omit the latter-clause relation marker (“但是、所以、就、才、也、还、却”); “因为、虽然、如果” may drop. Add a former-clause marker only when the relation needs to show early. No mechanical repeat of a connective, no filler such as “值得注意的是” in place of a relation, no vague clause-initial “而、则、并、且” (references/connectives.md 6).
  - In a paragraph opening and a node's opening sentence, show the relation at the very start, as the node's 接前 slot sets it. The relation is to the level above and to the sibling it follows. Use a relation connective, the answer and then “但是” for the limit, or a naming back-reference (the user's two edits of 2026-10-14: references/examples-and-rewrites.md, Revision example 4; words: references/connectives.md 1). The sibling is the previous one unless an earlier one is named. At an opening, turn (light turns included), cause, condition and concession are always marked, except a straight continuation or a first child that states the heading's claim; echoing a few words is not marking. The level below rides preview and payoff (C20); after a lead's reader question (C03), open with a back-reference or an add-on marker.
  - Check: look first for missing inference, “但是”, progression, concession, explication, back-reference, “甚至”, “其实”; the first few characters of each opening sentence show both relations.
  - Check: each seam passes seven checks; fix the wording, never pad. 1 Preview and payoff (C20). 2 The word's class matches the 接前 slot (FICCStructure.md S90). 3 Back-test: a cause before “所以”, one layer deeper after “而且”, one dimension across “但是” (a 因为／所以 that only restates: FICCStructure.md S28). 4 Latter-clause markers present. 5 Antecedent (V53). 6 Hypotheticals stay hypothetical (V17). 7 Items after a colon (V19).
- **V53** **Keep a connective right next to its true antecedent.** An opening connective links to the previous paragraph's claim or close, not to its last example (above section level, to the whole preceding story); move an inserted sentence away, or use a named back-reference. Check: the sentence right before each opening connective is its true antecedent.
- **V54** **When one seam carries two layers of relation, mark the layer the 接前 slot names (FICCStructure.md S29); when two layers nest across two seams, the outer layer's word goes at the outer seam and the inner layer's word at the inner seam.**

#### Reference, given–new, parallels

- **V12** **Keep wording and reference consistent.** Adjacent sentences on one object use one name or key term, with no needless change of wording; rename the object after intervening material; a counted concept lists its members at first mention.
- **V23** **Keep the same name pointing to the same object.** Check: replace each “这、它、该情况、上述” with the full name; where two objects fit, name it (“这笔贷款”); where none fits, reintroduce it.
- **V24** **Lead from an established object to the next new information.** Carry over from what the draft already says (notes the reader has not seen are new), then add the state, action or limit; introduce a new object and its role before its attributes; times, conditions and relation markers may come first, but build no passive for word order. The subject is the thing judged, by its full name; hang no topic and then switch, and open with no embedded question (“X和Y是什么关系，材料没说”); a gap names the judgment it limits first (analysing the sources themselves, the sources may be the topic). Check: the reader knows each name's referent and which earlier point each sentence picks up.
- **V25** **Use similar syntax to show items that truly correspond.** Write in parallel only items settled as one level, dimension and basis (FICCStructure.md S24, S19, S14; size S25), in one order; keep extra conditions, state gaps, invent nothing for symmetry; tie a long group back to the question.

#### Notes, figures, terms

- **V29** **Decide first whether it is a note, then where to put the parenthetical.** A note identifies, glosses, aliases or sources a word and adds no premise. Separate main and subordinate between sentences. A note does not count as a step in a sentence group. Attach it once where it serves, never as a sentence of its own (notes on two words: two places), after the word (else after the 。); basis, sample and period ride the number first; a figure's basis goes in its note. A note the next sentence builds on is an argument step: unfold it, and hide no premise in parentheses. Check: drop each note and read on; if later text needs it, or “样本内” reads as “全部” or “可能” as “确定”, move it back into the judgment.
- **V31** **Make figure, figure note, and body text match.** Prefer a title that states a finding the data support; figure, note and body share object, period and baseline. The body's use of the figure: C22.
- **V32** **Explain the one point the reader lacks. Do not let a term stand in for a mechanism.** For the V34 reader, explain only this piece's definitions, bases and ambiguous words, at first use, and stop. Use few terms: the logic in everyday words first, few new nouns per paragraph, no encyclopedia entry. Names come from the name table (FICCOutline.md O32–O33) and keep market names (沿用市场叫法); a new name goes back per V04, never coined; no coined label for an indicator, series or group, at most one coined name per piece, introduced in the lead, explained on the spot and kept. Hard to explain: reasoning first, name after (“这种手法，我们叫做……”). State a mechanism once in full, then refer back (“这两条规则”); no “收敛、付价、标零、兑现” for economic facts, no “读数、口径” in place of numbers and method.
- **V33** **Choose terms, abbreviations, and names by whether the reader can identify the object.** The name table's ruling and its language rule (FICCOutline.md O32–O33) come first, then the word readers grasp faster; never send the reader back for a definition. Lecture terms: references/lecture-terms.md.

#### Audience, persona, register

- **V34** **Write for the default audience in the right writing persona.** The default audience is practitioners with a master's background in finance or economics who know basic financial concepts. This is the cold-read bar of references/clarity-acceptance.md A01. Publish as the author, in your own style. “我” for real first-hand experience, “我们” by default in argument, “你” when working through the reader's choice; state any change of condition or persona. In someone else's name, take that persona and invent no trades, positions, research visits, client conversations or past judgments. Say that a hypothetical scene is hypothetical.
- **V35** **Make metaphors, characters, and jokes serve the current question.** Mechanism first, then a metaphor for the channel; an analogy states its limits and yields no conclusion the material lacks. Hooks: C21. Aim criticism at claims and mechanisms, never at client losses.
- **V36** **Convert to written register while keeping information, analysis, and personality. Do not write officialese.** Remove classroom cues and useless restatement (lecture notes may keep more characters and dialogue); set no pronoun minimum or joke quota; use parallelism and strong words only where content needs them, and give “不是……而是” concrete grounds. Sentences the author designated verbatim stay. Transcripts: references/written-register.md.

## 4 Check before handover and bounded patches

Run the Check lines of C01–C22 and C24 on the paragraph draft and those of §3 on its sentences, then C23 on the whole draft, then V37–V40. Before handover, run `scripts/voice_check.py --clarity --names` on the node and handle its flags per V39; a hard G-NEWNUM or G-DERIV from `scripts/inline_guards.py` goes back per V26.

A later check (check mode, or a check after a revision) covers the paragraphs changed and every paragraph whose opening, carry-over or close the change touched; paragraphs untouched since the last check keep that check's result, which the packet attaches. A whole-draft assembly that changes carry-overs, openings or closes re-runs C23 on the affected range only.

### 4.1 Openings and the finished-draft reads

- **C23** **Before submitting, check the openings at every layer again on the finished draft.**
  - With only the finished draft and the name table, extract each paragraph's opening (where it is: C01) and restate how the piece moves from one judgment to the next.
  - Check: cover everything after the opening and read only the opening; it passes when it states what its C01 paragraph-type row requires. Then read only the first sentence of the lead, of each part or section, and of each paragraph (or the sentence where its claim lands): each states the unit's whole settled claim as its C01 row requires (row 8: across its two sentences), with no sample list, gloss, source date or roadmap.
  - If you can restate only an attitude, a reading, a count, an evaluation, a subject, or what the block will discuss, rewrite it. One conclusion in the openings of more than two adjacent layers: revise per C07.
  - Check these first: a tone-only opening with no carry-over (C02); a carry-over written as contents or roadmap (C04, C06); counter-views or appendices in the carry-over paragraph (C05); a close that repeats or uplifts (C17, C19); carry-overs and closes forced onto small nodes (C19); a preview repeated as a later opening: shorten it, or let the opening add the relation word and the step (C04, C20).
- **V37** **First use the finished draft to test whether the reader must do organising work.**
  - With only the finished draft and the name table, state each sentence's object, judgment, conditions and the layer it serves; each place shows what is said now, why here, and how the text that follows makes it clear. Check: after a passage you can state its main meaning and why the next sentence connects; if not, fix mixed layers, stray material, missing premises, false turns and unclear references before the wording, and every spot where responsibility needs rereading, a pronoun must be guessed, or the reader must supply causes.
  - Avoid AI tone: check each paragraph for six signs and fix them where they cluster (a single one goes by its own rule): roadmap sentences, C01, C06 (script H2); hollow claims, C02, V38, V36; template tone, C09, C11, C19; term pileups, V32; end-of-paragraph uplift, C17, C19 (script row 段末升华); transition fillers, C17, V22 (script row 本质上等五词).
- **V38** **Then check both ways, from material to new text and from new text to material.** Find each V02 meaning's new location; lose no judgment, fact, number, base period, condition, exception, negation, degree or attribution, and move none to another object. Every added action, causal word, generalisation or certainty has grounds. In split and merged sentences, a shared condition still governs all, no exception has spread, no responsibilities merged; then check subject–predicate fit, modifier placement and missing characters.
- **V39** **Handle script flags by comprehension obstacle, not by driving counts to zero.**
  - When a flag such as clause count or noun-string length fires, check for an interrupted core, a condition to match back or a modifier that reads two ways, and fix that; if you can name no misreading or load, keep the text and say why. Never lower counts by cutting qualifiers, hiding them in parentheses or abbreviating.
  - Paragraph flags follow C08. H10 (more than 10 sentences) and the 400-CJK paragraph hint are review hints like the flags above. A paragraph with ≥30 number strings gets `重段待处置`, which must be disposed of before handover: table the isomorphic grid, split at a sub-question or basis seam with 拆后四带, or attach a verifier-signed proof of one indivisible claim (barred when two denominators mix). An undisposed `重段待处置` does not pass.
- **V40** **Report reading problems that can be located and fixed.** Cold-read your own node before handover with only the finished draft and the name table (the cold read after assembly: references/clarity-acceptance.md A01). A cold read of another's draft reports only: no replacement sentence, direction, cause, certainty, holder or unit. Per spot give node, line, original sentence, rule, the likely misreading or look-back, and where the text falls short; mark A for a likely misreading, B for slowed reading, never just “长、绕、不顺”. In your own node, fix both grades before handover, hand uncertain ones back per V04, and give a reason for each false alarm.

### 4.2 Bounded patches (修补纪律)

The writer's side of a patch; the checker's side is references/clarity-acceptance.md A02.

- Under a grant limited to splitting or merging paragraphs, add no sentences and keep sentence order; a paragraph left without its claim at the opening is reported as a to-do, not written.
- A patch touches only the sentences and to-dos the packet names, plus the opening sentence of their paragraph where the change directly affects it.
- A pure B-grade patch adds zero net CJK characters to the section, in every attempt.
- A new derived number is not computed in the patch: it goes back to the living outline as a 算式 row (V26).
- Every new sentence, the 主持席's own hand edits included, re-passes five gates: value, adjacent unit, holder, direction word, condition polarity (never “或”→“和”).
- A numeric or basis difference is cleared only by the verifier's sign-off against the source line, never by the writer's argument.
- Result sheet: each change as file:line before → after.
- **V14** **Revise by problem type.** Fix sentence by sentence: an unclear subject, state it; an unclear condition, scope or exception, complete it next to what it qualifies; several judgments in one sentence, split them with their qualifiers; an unclear link back, check for missing information and order (V13); wordy but complete, cut redundancy. The output need not show the check.
